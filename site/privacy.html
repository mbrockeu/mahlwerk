Privacy policy of the iPhone app

Last updated: 26 November 2026. The German version is the binding one.

This policy covers the iPhone app “Mahlwerk”, including test builds distributed through TestFlight. In short: the app keeps your data on your iPhone. There is no account with the provider, no server run by the provider, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The provider never sees your diary.

2. Data on your iPhone

What you enter is stored in a database inside the app on your iPhone: meals and foods, water, weight and body values, activity, sleep, workouts, fasting, recipes, templates, goals and settings. The Home Screen widget reads today's totals (calories, water) from an area on the device that only the app can access. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can export it to a file at any time from the profile.

3. Apple Health (optional)

Only if you turn it on in the app and allow it in iOS, Mahlwerk reads values from Apple Health: weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass, water, steps, active energy, distance (walking and running, for workouts also cycling and swimming), sleep and workouts. If you also allow writing, the app adds your daily energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat and water from the diary to Apple Health and keeps these values up to date. The app uses these values only to show and analyse them for you in Mahlwerk. They are not used for advertising or marketing, not sold and not shared with third parties. They are not part of the iCloud backup (section 4). You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Health app or in the iOS settings.

4. iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on the backup, the app stores a backup file in the iCloud Drive of your Apple Account (folder “Mahlwerk”). It contains your entries, recipes, templates, goals and settings. Values from Apple Health and stored credentials (Cookidoo) are not included. The file is held by Apple under the iCloud terms; the provider of Mahlwerk has no access to it. You can turn the backup off at any time and delete the files in the Files app.

5. Camera

The app uses the camera only when you open it: to read barcodes and to take photos of meals, nutrition labels or recipes. The app analyses photos on the device, with the text recognition of iOS and Apple's on-device language model. It does not store the photos, and they do not leave the device.

6. Connections to the internet

  • Open Food Facts: when you scan or enter a barcode that is not yet stored in the app, the app asks Open Food Facts (France) for the product data. The barcode and, as with any internet connection, your IP address are sent. The answer is then stored on the device.
  • Food catalogue: when you download or update the catalogue, the file comes from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA). Your IP address is sent; no diary data is.
  • Cookidoo (optional): if you connect your Cookidoo account, the app talks to Cookidoo (Vorwerk) to load your recipes. Your credentials are stored encrypted on the device, with the key in the iOS keychain.
  • Yazio import: a Yazio export file is read on the device; nothing is sent.

The app makes no other connections, and it contacts third parties only when you use the feature.

7. Using your own server (optional)

You can also connect the app to a Mahlwerk server run by you or someone else. The app then stores your data on that server instead of the iPhone, and whoever runs the server is responsible for it. The access key is kept in the iOS keychain.

8. TestFlight

If you take part in a TestFlight test, Apple gives the provider your name, your email address, the build you use and the crash reports and feedback you send through TestFlight, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy terms. The provider uses this only to run the test and deletes it afterwards.

9. Legal bases

Where the provider processes any of your data at all (TestFlight, email enquiries), this is to run the test or answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). The app processes Health values and other health information only on your device and only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent. Please write to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example [SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY].

11. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver it. The site sets no cookies, loads nothing from other websites and does not track you.

12. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy is updated. The version with the latest date applies.
